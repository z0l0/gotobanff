BANFF HOT SPRINGS

When to go, what to bring, how to avoid peak crowds, and where it fits in a Banff trip.

Updated 2026 planning guide

THE SHORT ANSWER

Best use: Banff Upper Hot Springs is a strong cold-weather, rest-day, or bad-weather activity.

Check first: hours, admission, capacity, and maintenance closures can change.

Trip fit: pair it with the gondola/Sulphur Mountain area or a lower-effort town day.

WHEN HOT SPRINGS MAKE SENSE

Use it intentionally, not as a filler.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Fast answers for planning this part of Banff.

Are Banff Hot Springs worth it?

Yes for winter, rest days, and bad weather. If you want solitude, avoid peak times and check current capacity or maintenance notices.

Do I need to book Banff Hot Springs?

Check the official hot springs site for current admission, hours, and operating rules before going.

Can I combine hot springs with the Banff Gondola?

Yes. Both are in the Sulphur Mountain area, making them a natural pairing if the timing and budget work.
