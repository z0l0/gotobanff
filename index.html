PICK YOUR SEASON

Each season has its own magic (and challenges)

Peak Season

SUMMER (JUN-AUG)

Warm days, all trails open, turquoise lakes at their best. But expect crowds.

Sweet Spot

FALL (SEP-OCT)

Golden larches, fewer crowds, crisp air. Our favorite time to visit.

Adventure Mode

WINTER (NOV-MAR)

World-class skiing, frozen waterfalls, northern lights. Requires preparation.

Transition

SPRING (APR-MAY)

Melting snow, wildlife emerging, unpredictable weather. Budget-friendly.

BANFF PLANNING AT A GLANCE

The fixed pieces to solve before you start adding hikes and viewpoints

Park admission Daily adult admission is $12.25 in 2026, with free admission listed for June 19 to September 7 under Canada Strong Pass.
Lake shuttles Lake Louise and Moraine Lake shuttle reservations open April 15, 2026, with additional seats released 48 hours ahead.
Moraine Lake Personal vehicles are not permitted on Moraine Lake Road. Plan shuttle, transit, or licensed commercial transport.
Winter roads Use 511 Alberta before driving and know that the Icefields Parkway has winter tire or chain requirements in the winter window.

GETTING TO BANFF

From Calgary Airport to mountain paradise

Winter Driving Advisory

Highway 1 between Calgary and Banff can be icy, snowy, and slow in winter. Alberta recommends winter tires for winter highway driving, and Parks Canada requires snow tires or chains on the Icefields Parkway during the winter window. Take the shuttle if you do not want winter-road stress.
