BANFF PARK PASS

Yes, you need one. Here's the deal.

Updated from Parks Canada 2026 fees

THE BASICS

Do I need a pass? Yes. Banff is a national park and requires a valid Parks Canada pass.

Daily pass: $12.25 CAD/adult, free for youth (17 and under)

Discovery Pass: $83.50 CAD/adult - unlimited entry to Parks Canada destinations for a year

Special 2026 note: Parks Canada lists free admission from June 19 to September 7, 2026 under the Canada Strong Pass program. Service fees such as shuttle reservations, parking, camping, and hot springs still apply.

Where to buy: Park gates, visitor centres, or online through Parks Canada

PASS OPTIONS

Which one makes sense for you

DAILY PASS

$12.25 CAD
  • Valid for one day at one park
  • Adult (18-64): $12.25
  • Senior (65+): $10.75
  • Youth (17 & under): FREE
  • Family/Group (up to 7): $24.50

Best for: Day trips, short visits (1-3 days)

BEST VALUE

DISCOVERY PASS

$83.50 CAD
  • Valid for 12 months from purchase
  • Unlimited entry to ALL Parks Canada sites
  • Adult (18-64): $83.50
  • Senior (65+): $71.50
  • Family/Group: $167.50

Best for: Stays of 7+ days, visiting multiple parks (Jasper, Yoho, etc.)

💡 The math: Pays for itself in just 7 days!

WHERE TO BUY

Multiple options, all easy

🌐

Online (Recommended)

Buy through Parks Canada before you go. Print or save to your phone. Skip the line at the gate.

🚗

Park Gate

Stop at the east gate on Highway 1 when entering from Calgary. Can get busy in summer - expect lines.

🏢

Visitor Centres

Banff Visitor Centre on Banff Avenue. Also at Lake Louise Visitor Centre.

🏨

Some Hotels

Many hotels in Banff sell passes at the front desk. Ask when you check in.

💡 Pro Tip: Buy online before your trip. The park gate can have 20-30 minute waits in peak summer. Your pass is valid from the date you choose, not the purchase date.

GOOD TO KNOW

The fine print

Do I need a pass just to drive through?

Yes. Even if you're just passing through on Highway 1, you need a valid pass. The highway runs through the park.

Where do I display my pass?

Hang it from your rearview mirror or place it on your dashboard, visible from outside. Rangers do check.

Does my pass work at Jasper, Yoho, etc.?

Daily passes are park-specific. The Discovery Pass works at ALL Parks Canada locations across the country.

What if I take the shuttle from Calgary?

You still need a pass. The shuttle doesn't include park admission. Buy online before you arrive.

What's the fine for not having a pass?

Up to $25,000 under the Canada National Parks Act. Not worth the risk - just buy the pass!
