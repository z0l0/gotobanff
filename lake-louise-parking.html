LAKE LOUISE PARKING

Paid lots, free Park and Ride choices, RV rules, shuttle backups, and timing decisions.

Updated 2026 planning guide

THE SHORT ANSWER

Yes, you can park at Lake Louise, but peak-season parking is limited and paid at the Lakeshore and Fairview day-use area.

2026 paid parking window: Parks Canada lists paid parking from May 15 to October 12, 3 am to 7 pm.

Best low-stress option: reserve a Parks Canada shuttle and park free at the Lake Louise Park and Ride at the ski resort.

WHEN TO AVOID DRIVING UP

These are the moments where shuttle logic wins.

Peak summer

Use Park and Ride

If you are not arriving very early, the Park and Ride plus shuttle is usually less stressful.

RV / oversized

Do not assume lake parking

Parks Canada restricts oversized private vehicles at the lakeshore; shuttle parking is the safer plan.

Free admission dates

Parking still matters

Free park admission does not remove Lake Louise parking fees or shuttle reservation needs.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Fast answers for planning this part of Banff.

Is Lake Louise parking free?

Some parking is free, including the Park and Ride for shuttle passengers and some community parking. Lakeshore and Fairview paid parking apply during the 2026 shuttle season.

Can I reserve Lake Louise parking?

Parks Canada says parking permits are purchased on-site and valid only on the day of purchase; shuttle reservations are separate.

Does a Discovery Pass include Lake Louise parking?

No. Parks Canada states Lake Louise parking is separate from park admission and Discovery Passes.
