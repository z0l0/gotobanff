LAKE LOUISE & MORAINE LAKE SHUTTLE 2026

The 2026 planning guide for shuttles, parking, timing, and backup options.

Updated for 2026 shuttle and fee rules

THE SHORT ANSWER

Best plan for most visitors: reserve the Parks Canada shuttle from the Lake Louise Park and Ride, then use the Lake Connector between Lake Louise and Moraine Lake.

Critical rule: personal vehicles are not permitted on Moraine Lake Road. Do not build your day around driving there.

Reservation timing: 2026 shuttle reservations open April 15 at 8 am MT for 40% of seats; the rest release 48 hours before departure at 8 am MT.

Parking reality: paid parking is in effect at Lake Louise Lakeshore and Fairview from May 15 to October 12, 2026, 3 am to 7 pm.

Source check: Shuttle reservation windows, Park and Ride rules, Moraine Lake vehicle restrictions, and paid parking dates are from Parks Canada. Always re-check before booking because shuttle operations can change with conditions.

THE BOOKING PATH

A simple sequence for the least stressful lake day.

If You Want Both Lakes

  1. Create or test your Parks Canada Reservation Service account before launch day.
  2. Choose your first lake carefully; Parks Canada says your initial destination must match your reservation.
  3. Check in at the Lake Louise Park and Ride at 1 Whitehorn Road during your one-hour window.
  4. Use the Lake Connector between Lake Louise and Moraine Lake once you have your official shuttle ticket.
  5. Keep your return plan flexible; crowds and weather can slow everything down.

If Reservations Sell Out

  • Try the 48-hour rolling release at 8 am MT.
  • Check Roam transit options from Banff.
  • Use a licensed commercial tour or private shuttle if the schedule works.
  • Shift to Lake Minnewanka, Johnston Canyon, Tunnel Mountain, or Vermilion Lakes instead of forcing a bad parking plan.
  • Do not drive to Moraine Lake Road hoping for an exception.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Quick answers for the shuttle questions that usually derail planning.

Can I see Lake Louise and Moraine Lake on the same shuttle ticket?

Yes, if you have an eligible Parks Canada shuttle ticket and check in properly at the Park and Ride, your reservation includes the Lake Connector between the two lakes for that date.

Should I choose Lake Louise or Moraine Lake as my first destination?

Choose the lake you genuinely want to visit first. Parks Canada says your first shuttle from the Park and Ride must go to the initial destination selected on your reservation.

What if I only get a late shuttle time?

A late shuttle can still work well for photos and shorter walks, especially when day-trippers begin leaving. Avoid pairing a late departure with a long hike unless you have checked return times.

Do kids need shuttle tickets?

Yes. Parks Canada lists youth shuttle fares separately from adult and senior fares. Youth park admission is free, but service fees can still apply.
