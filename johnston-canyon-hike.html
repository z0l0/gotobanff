JOHNSTON CANYON HIKE

Lower Falls, Upper Falls, Ink Pots, winter traction, parking, transit, and timing choices.

Updated 2026 planning guide

THE SHORT ANSWER

Best first-time option: hike at least to Lower Falls; continue to Upper Falls if time, energy, and conditions are good.

Longer day: Ink Pots adds distance and a very different open-valley finish beyond the canyon.

Winter caveat: traction can be essential. Check Parks Canada trail conditions before treating winter photos as easy.

HOW TO PLAN THE DAY

The canyon is easy to start and easy to underestimate.

Timing

Go early or late

Crowds change the experience more than the trail difficulty.

Winter

Bring traction

Ice cleats or microspikes can matter on packed snow and icy catwalks.

Transit

Check Route 9

Roam’s Johnston Canyon service can reduce parking stress when operating.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Fast answers for planning this part of Banff.

Is Johnston Canyon an easy hike?

Lower Falls is one of the easier classic Banff hikes, but winter ice, crowds, and continuing to Upper Falls or Ink Pots increase the commitment.

Is Upper Falls worth it?

Yes for many visitors if conditions are good and you have the time. It is a better canyon day than stopping only at the first viewpoint.

Can you visit Johnston Canyon in winter?

Often yes, but check official trail conditions and bring traction when the path is icy.
