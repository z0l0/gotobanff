ROAM TRANSIT BANFF

How to use Banff buses for car-free days, lake logistics, Canmore links, and backup planning.

Updated 2026 planning guide

THE SHORT ANSWER

Roam is best for car-light Banff plans, especially town routes, Canmore-Banff links, Lake Louise express service, Johnston Canyon service, and seasonal local routes.

Do not treat every route as year-round. Check the live Roam schedule before building a no-car itinerary.

For Moraine Lake, understand the difference between Roam Super Pass access and Parks Canada shuttle reservations.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Fast answers for planning this part of Banff.

Can you visit Banff without a car using Roam?

Yes for many core sights, especially if you stay in Banff town. You still need to check route seasonality, reservations, and return times.

Does Roam go to Lake Louise?

Roam lists Lake Louise-Banff Express Route 8X; check the current schedule and reservation/pass rules before relying on it.

Does Roam go to Johnston Canyon?

Roam lists Johnston Canyon Regional Route 9, but operating dates and times should be checked before planning the day.
