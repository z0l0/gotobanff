BANFF CAMPGROUNDS

Compare the main Banff campground options before reservations disappear.

Updated 2026 planning guide

THE SHORT ANSWER

Best town access: Tunnel Mountain campgrounds are closest to Banff services and transit.

Best lake feel: Two Jack Lakeside is scenic and popular but smaller.

Official rule: Parks Canada says camping requires a camping permit and national park entry pass, and camping is only permitted in designated areas.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Fast answers for planning this part of Banff.

Do Banff campgrounds require reservations?

Parks Canada says visitors are required to have a reservation to camp at Parks Canada places. Some campgrounds also have first-come-first-served periods; confirm current details.

Do I need a park pass to camp in Banff?

Yes. Parks Canada says campers need a national park entry pass plus a camping permit.

Can I camp anywhere in Banff?

No. Camping is permitted only in designated campgrounds or backcountry sites with the appropriate permit.
