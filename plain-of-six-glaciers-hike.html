Is the Plain of Six Glaciers hike worth it?

Yes if it matches your fitness, access plan, season, and weather window. Lake Louise access, weather, and daylight matter.

Do I need a shuttle for Plain of Six Glaciers?

Lake Louise and Moraine Lake area hikes often need a shuttle, transit, parking, or licensed-operator plan in peak season. Tunnel Mountain is town-adjacent.
