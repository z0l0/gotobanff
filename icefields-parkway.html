ICEFIELDS PARKWAY

A practical drive guide for stops, timing, fuel, weather, winter rules, and Banff-Jasper planning.

Updated 2026 planning guide

THE SHORT ANSWER

Do not treat the Icefields Parkway as a commute. It is a scenic mountain drive with limited services and weather exposure.

Best use: make it a full daylight travel day between Banff/Lake Louise and Jasper, or a shorter out-and-back sample from Lake Louise.

Winter caveat: check 511 Alberta and Parks Canada conditions before departure; services and traction requirements matter.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Fast answers for planning this part of Banff.

Can you drive the Icefields Parkway in one day?

Yes, but it is best treated as a full scenic day, especially if driving between Banff or Lake Louise and Jasper.

Is the Icefields Parkway safe in winter?

It can be, but only with appropriate vehicle preparation, current road checks, conservative timing, and comfort with mountain winter driving.

Where should I check conditions?

Use 511 Alberta for road status and Parks Canada for closures, warnings, and winter driving guidance.
